      Dengue: know the warning signs

      Go to hospital immediately if you have

      • Severe stomach pain or vomiting
      • Bleeding gums or nose
      • Feeling very tired or restless

      Fever with headache, pain behind the eyes and body aches can be dengue. Drink plenty of fluids and see a doctor within 1–2 days.
